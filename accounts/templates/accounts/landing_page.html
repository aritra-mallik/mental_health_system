Frequently Asked Questions

Is this therapy? +

No. SupportSync is a digital support and awareness platform. It is not a replacement for professional clinical care, therapy, or medical advice. Think of it as a compassionate first step.

Is my data safe? +

Absolutely. We utilize zero-knowledge architecture. All conversations and journals are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256-GCM before syncing.

Can I delete my data? +

Yes. Because we use a zero-knowledge architecture, your data lives securely on your device. You maintain full control and can clear your local storage at any time to completely erase your history.

How does Emotion AI work? +

Our AI analyzes the textual sentiment of your entries to recognize underlying emotions. It uses this context to provide more empathetic, tailored guidance while keeping your actual data entirely private.

Is it free to use? +

Yes — core features including the mood check-in and WHO-5 assessment are completely free. You can use it anonymously without an account.

What if I am in a crisis? +

SupportSync is not a crisis hotline. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency or having thoughts of self-harm, please contact your local emergency services or a dedicated crisis lifeline immediately.

⚠️ Important Notice

SupportSync is a well-being awareness and guidance platform — not a substitute for professional clinical care, therapy, or medical advice. If you are in crisis, please contact a qualified mental health professional or a local emergency service immediately.
