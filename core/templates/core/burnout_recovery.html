Burnout Recovery

You are running on empty. The goal today isn't productivity; it's gentle, guilt-free restoration.

⚠️

Permission to Rest

Burnout is a physiological state, not a lack of willpower. If you cannot do any of the tasks below, your only task today is to rest. Your body needs safety to heal.

Micro-Activations (The Freeze Breaker)

When everything feels too heavy, pick just ONE micro-task. Completely ignore the rest.

💧

Hydrate

Drink exactly one glass of water. Notice the cool temperature. That is your only goal right now.

🌤️

Change Scenery

Move from the bed to the couch, or open a single window to feel the fresh air on your face.

🧠

Brain Dump

Write down 3 things weighing heavily on your mind, fold the paper, and physically put it away.

🎧

Sensory Shift

Play one familiar, calming song. Close your eyes and just listen. Do not look at a screen.

Energy Pacing Rule (The 50% Rule)

When recovering from burnout, your perception of your energy is skewed. Whatever you think you have the energy to do today—cut it in half immediately.
